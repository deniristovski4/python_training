Avenga is a business software company founded by Igor Lestar, Richard Murbeck and Gligor Dacevski in 1999 in Malmö.[2][3]
In 2003, the company released the first version of Avenga Project Viewer.[3]
In 2004, Avenga opened its first office in the United States in an effort to expand beyond Europe and enter the markets in North and South America.[3]
In 2009, the company released the first versions of Avenga Project Planner and Savus DropMind; DropMind was later rebranded as iMindQ in 2014.[3]
In 2012, Avenga introduced Crystal Qube at the Mobile World Congress in Barcelona, Spain.[3] In the same year, BestAppEver ranked Avenga' iOS games Yatzy Ultimate and Black Jack Ultimate third place for the best dice game and the best casino game, respectively.[4][5]
In 2013, the company's latest solution on gaming platforms and payment methods, Avenga Guardians, was presented at the 12th European iGaming Congress&Expo (EiG) 2013[6]—the largest European gaming event in Barcelona. Furthermore, in only one month, an additional 6 games were launched by the Avenga Gaming team.
In 2015, the Avenga Gaming team launched Yatzi Ultimate for Android. In the same year, Avenga continued its growth on the Scandinavian market through the acquisition of OnTrax AB,[7] a Stockholm-based IT Consultancy Company.
In 2016, Avenga incubator[8] launched iThink, an operation for accelerating visionary young startup companies. iThink was marketed as the first technology academy for children in Macedonia started working as part of Avenga Education and Development Center. The first version of ScheduleReader was also launched in 2016.
In 2017, Avenga DOOEL was certified as a Cisco Select Certified Partner Partner in EUROPE EAST.[9] In January, "Ontrax AB" became Avenga Stockholm AB.
In 2021, Avenga became partners with Salesforce.[10]
In June 2023, Avenga, Cleverlance, Komix Digital, Stratiteq, Clearcode, and Musala Soft merged to create Qinshift with 3,000 IT specialists.[11][12]
